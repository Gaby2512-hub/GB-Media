Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Before publication

If you cancel before your campaign has been approved and published, you may request cancellation. Refund eligibility depends on work already completed and applicable law.

After publication

Once a campaign has started, fees are generally non-refundable for services already delivered, except where required by law or where GB Media cannot provide the agreed service.

Rejected adverts

If an advert is rejected before publication and no suitable alternative can be offered, payment for the undelivered campaign will be reviewed for refund.

How to request a cancellation

Contact GB Media through the website as soon as possible and include your name, business name and campaign details.
